## Context

Blankos Block Party is a web3 open-world action platformer. Players battle, race, and socialise across maps built by the dev team and the community.

Worked at Third Kind Games in partnership with Mythical Games.

## What I owned

Senior Game Designer. Improved and developed new core gameplay experiences, and supported Live Ops events and content.

- Outlining and implementing season-based challenges, experiences, and rewards.
- Working with UI/UX disciplines on matchmaking and onboarding.

## Case study: Battle Pass

Designed and implemented the Battle Pass system on a monthly cadence, with Free and Premium reward tracks.

<Image src={battlePass} alt="Blankos Battle Pass" />

The work surfaced three challenges.

### 1. Balancing progression

**Problem.** Players needed meaningful, achievable goals per season, enough to drive daily/weekly engagement, without exhausting content too early or feeling blocked behind monetisation walls.

**Approach.**

- Analysed daily playtime and challenge completion rates to set XP targets and challenge quantities per tier.
- Categorised challenges by difficulty, time investment, and gameplay variety, mixing quick wins with longer-term goals.
- Defined XP pacing curves for the monthly cadence, allowing completion with consistent play and leaving stretch goals for high-engagement users.

**Outcome.** A progression curve that incentivised consistent play without overwhelming casual players. Free Track players felt rewarded; Premium upgrades motivated by additional cosmetics and NFTs.

### 2. NFT drops tied to progression

**Problem.** NFTs as Battle Pass rewards meant synchronising design and deployment pipelines, balancing scarcity, player progression rates, and blockchain minting and drop-timing constraints.

**Approach.**

- Collaborated with Mythical Games' blockchain team to understand minting timelines and constraints.
- Designed NFT drops as mid to late-tier Battle Pass rewards to drive retention through the season.
- Aligned supply with projected player completion rates.

**Outcome.** Smooth NFT deployment tied to clear progression milestones, with supply matched to projected completion rates.

### 3. Connecting challenges and progress in the UI

**Problem.** Early Battle Pass and challenge UI felt disjointed. Players struggled to see how challenges contributed to progression, undermining engagement and clarity.

<Image src={challenges} alt="Blankos challenges screen" />

**Approach.**

- Ran UX discussions and player-flow reviews to identify friction points.
- Wireframed: integrated progress bars on challenge cards showing XP contribution; clearer tier milestones with preview rewards; reorganised challenge categories for easier navigation.
- Iterated wireframes into final in-game designs with the UI/UX team, aligning to brand style and technical feasibility.

**Outcome.** Challenge completion felt directly tied to Battle Pass progression. Daily challenge engagement and reward-claim rates both increased.
